## KPIs bloqueados

- `yoy_growth_pct`: Requires comparable source periods for current and prior year.
- `historical_growth_pct`: Requires at least two comparable source periods.
- `geographic_spend_pen`: Official governed UBIGEO dimension is not loaded.
- `final_value_pen`: Final contract value coverage is only 0.2681 percent.
- `supplier_market_share`: Deferred to Phase 11 market concentration.
- `b2g_opportunity_score`: Deferred to Phase 12 methodology and sensitivity analysis.
- `supplier_exposure_score`: Deferred to Phase 13 methodology and sensitivity analysis.

## Reconciliación

Las 7 métricas críticas coinciden con la evidencia de Fase 9. No se calculan crecimiento, YoY, HHI ni scores en esta fase.
